First — this is real

This isn't jealousy. It's distance, given a face.

Watching someone who shares almost everything I love — the F1, the hometown, all of it — and then watching him just be there. After work. Every evening. In the same city as you. That's not about wanting him.

That's about wanting me — except today the wanting had a name and a face sitting a few desks away.

That ache is real, and it's allowed. You're not being petty, or jealous, or "too much." You're just feeling, very vividly, the exact thing distance keeps trying to make you forget you're missing.

But here's the thing

Similar isn't the same.
